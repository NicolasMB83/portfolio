WORK

Colombia 4.0

The event where the creative industry meets digital technology. I was part of the design team, where I developed graphic content based on the official key visual.

YEAR
2018
COMPANY
Colombian Ministry of ICT (MINTIC)
ROLE
Digital Graphic Designer

Objectives and challenges

  1. Brand Cohesion

    Designed graphic elements that maintained the essence of Colombia 4.0.

  2. Event Coverage

    Created real-time graphics and social media posts during the event.

  3. Agenda and Guests

    Supported agenda development and designed materials for invited speakers.

My approach

Discovery Phase

  • Graphic Concept Ideation

    I developed graphic proposals for the event Colombia 4.0.

  • Concept Development

    Based on the selected graphic proposal, the construction of the graphic universe and its applications across different media begins.

Design and Development

  • Development of Pieces for Social Media

    Designed promotional materials for social media before, during, and after the event.

  • Agenda and Speakers Design

    Part of COl40’s success lies in the quality of its speakers, bringing in current references from the creative world, designing promotional pieces for the speakers was a fundamental part of promoting the event on social media.

  • Event and management

    During the event, additional material were required for promotion at key moments, photos during presentations to encourage potential attendees to interact and boost social media engagement and metrics.

Social media, speakers and the event

Results

+62,000 attendees

Colombia 4.0 sustained its position as the leading technology and digital economy event in Latin America, attracting over 62,000 attendees. Topics included AI, software development, animation, video games, music, and entrepreneurship. Graphic assets for the event were key to conveying the vibrant and innovative identity, and I subsequently supported the 2019, 2020, and 2021 editions by creating graphic materials for social media along the whole Ministry of ICT design team.
