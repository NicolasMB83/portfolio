# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Recruiters and in-house hiring managers** (Colombia, US, remote teams) evaluating whether Nicolás is senior enough for a visual / product / digital design role. They arrive from LinkedIn, a CV or an application link, often on a laptop during work hours, sometimes on a phone between meetings. They skim for about a minute: who is he, what has he shipped, for whom, how senior.
- **Freelance clients** (brands, agencies, institutions) deciding whether to trust him with their identity, campaign or web project. They want proof of range and a direct way to start a conversation.

Job to be done: judge credibility fast, then either download the CV or start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio for Nicolás Mogollón Barbosa, a multidisciplinary designer (digital graphic design, web, UI, brand identity, AI-assisted visual design). It shows selected work (Radio a Domicilio, Invest in Bogotá, Vende Digital, Infinited, Colombia 4.0, Pro Surge JA), the services he offers, the organizations he has worked with and his career path.

Success: a visitor reaches the work within the first viewport, understands his seniority from the career path and client list, and acts (Let's talk / Download CV / View portfolio).

## Brand Personality

Solid, strategic, senior. The page should feel like the work of someone you can hand a brand to: composed, confident, precise in its use of color and type, with warmth carried by the work itself and the illustrations rather than by decoration.

Emotional goals: trust, clarity, quiet confidence. Never needy, never gimmicky.

## Anti-references

- Generic Framer / Webflow portfolio templates: pill nav floating over lavender, black caption bars on image cards, carousels with arrow buttons, infinite logo marquees.
- SaaS landing-page grammar: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, uppercase eyebrows above every section, gradient text, glass cards.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (italic display serif, drop caps, broadsheet rules) — this is a designer's portfolio, not a magazine.

## Design Principles

1. **Work first.** The projects are the proof; they appear early, large and uncluttered.
2. **Practice what you preach.** Every detail (type scale, spacing, motion, contrast) is a sample of the craft being sold.
3. **Seniority through restraint.** One idea per section, color used with intent, no ornament that does not carry meaning.
4. **Keep his voice.** The copy is his and stays untouched; design gives it structure and emphasis, never rewrites it.
5. **Two clear exits.** Talking to him and downloading the CV are always one obvious step away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, visible focus states, keyboard reachable navigation.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: animations become instant or static, Lottie covers stop on a still frame.
- Content stays visible without JavaScript; motion only enhances an already-visible default.
- Works from 320px phones to wide desktops without horizontal scroll.
